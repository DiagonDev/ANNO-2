The forward pass through an $L$-layer network is defined as:
\begin{align*}
    \mathbf{h}^{(0)} &= \mathbf{x} \\
    \mathbf{h}^{(l)} &= g(\mathbf{W}^{(l)\top} \mathbf{h}^{(l-1)} + \mathbf{b}^{(l)}), \quad l = 1, \dots, L - 1 \\
    \hat{\mathbf{y}} &= o(\mathbf{W}^{(L)\top} \mathbf{h}^{(L-1)} + \mathbf{b}^{(L)})
\end{align*}
\newline
The last pre-activation, $\mathbf{z}^{(L)} = \mathbf{W}^{(L)\top} \mathbf{h}^{(L-1)} + \mathbf{b}^{(L)}$, is called the vector of \textbf{logits}; the output function $o$ (identity, sigmoid or softmax) turns it into the prediction.

\begin{definition}[Number of parameters]
Layer $l$ has $d_{l-1} \cdot d_l$ weights and $d_l$ biases:
\begin{equation}
    |\theta| = \sum_{l=1}^{L} (d_{l-1} + 1) d_l
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
Most parameters sit in the first layer. In Fig \ref{tab:mnist_parameters} there is an example of 
the calculus of parameters. The MLP is : $784 \to 256 \to 128 \to 10$. 
Input: $28 \times 28 = 784$ pixels. Output: $10$ classes.
